A cemetery route puzzle

Inspecting the Cemetery

Help Simon trace today’s inspection route through the gravestones.

A different route every day

The cemetery behind the castle needs to be inspected daily—especially for gravestones that may have become unstable or fallen over.

Simon Simpson, the Cemetery Supervisor, chose a different route each day, purely to bring some variety into his otherwise boring life. One day his wife Celia set him a challenge: she drew a grid, added segments of a route, then wrote how many route pieces belonged in each row and column.

Can you help Simon trace the right path?

How to play: Make one continuous route from S to F. Choose a tile, then select a square. Select the same straight or corner tile again to rotate it. The numbers above and beside the grid tell you how many route squares belong in each column and row. Diamond-marked clues cannot be changed.

Check colours: green = exact, amber = correctly occupied but unresolved, red = incorrect.

Inspection complete!

Simon has followed the correct route and checked every part of the cemetery.
