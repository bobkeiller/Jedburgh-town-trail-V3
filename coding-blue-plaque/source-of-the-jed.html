The Source of the Jed

Tracing the source of the Jed is not easy.

High in the Cheviots, the Jed Water is formed by Raven Burn and Tod Sike. Raven Burn is formed by several smaller streams. “Burn” and “sike” mean “stream”.

Tracing the Tod Sike is tricky—can you find its path?

How to play: Build one continuous stream from J to S. Choose a tile and select a square; select it again to rotate it. Edge numbers give the stream-square totals. Use Check square to test one tile. Diamond clues are fixed.

Source found!

You have traced the Tod Sike back towards its source in the Cheviots.
