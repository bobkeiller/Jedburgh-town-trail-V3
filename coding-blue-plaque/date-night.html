Glebe Murder Part 3

Date Night

Reconstruct an anonymous supper and discover who wore the victim’s coat.

Who was the victim?

Police found a ticket in the victim’s coat pocket:

Royal Hotel
Date Night

They discovered that eight people had enjoyed supper together the night before as part of a dating club. Everything was anonymous to ensure discretion, so police could not trace the attendees.

The waitress’s statement

“There were eight seats at the table. We numbered places 1–4 along the wall and 5–8 facing them. The men sat with their backs to the wall, probably watching the door!”

“There was a Colin, Tom, Harry and Dick. The women were Pamela, Diane, Alison and Fiona. A brown duffle coat hung on Seat 1.”

How to play: Select a diner from the waiting list, then select their seat. Select × to clear a seat. Place everyone, then check your answer.

Reading the table: Seats 1–4 run left to right along the top, with their backs to the wall. Seats 5–8 face them and also run left to right on the diagram. Left and right in the clues are from each diner’s own seated point of view.

Clues

  1. Colin sat opposite Fiona.
  2. Pamela occupied one of the middle seats.
  3. Dick had someone on his right.
  4. A sparkly handbag was on Seat 6; the waitress later saw Pamela with it.
  5. Diane sat at an end, with all the other women to her right.
  6. Diane did not sit next to Fiona.
  7. Fiona was not in an end seat.
  8. Tom sat on Colin’s left.

Correct—Harry Page was in Seat 1

Harry Page told the waitress that after the bar closed, he’d be going to “look for salmon in the cauld at the Glebe”.

A cauld is a Scottish name for a weir. It is also a play on “cold” and “called”—although “called” is not an especially Scottish word.
