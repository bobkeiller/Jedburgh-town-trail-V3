Jedburgh Town Trail · Matrix puzzle

Lost Property

Three riders, three horses and three missing possessions. Can you discover who rode the black horse—and what they dropped?

The story

Three riders used the Loupin’-on Stane to mount their horses in 1799, and each accidentally dropped something. Use the clues to determine who rode the black horse and what that rider dropped.

People

  • Walter Scott
  • Sheriff Shortreed
  • Gladys Hogg

Horses

  • Brown
  • Black
  • White

Dropped objects

  • Coin
  • Watch
  • Brooch

The clues

  1. The coin was not dropped by the person on the brown horse.
  2. Mrs Hogg later discovered that she was missing an ornate brooch.
  3. Walter Scott never rode a white horse.
  4. Sheriff Robert Shortreed dropped his watch.
  5. Gladys Hogg wore a brown dress to match her horse.

How to complete the matrix

Each person rode only one horse and dropped only one object. Mark a known relationship with a tick where two elements intersect. A tick automatically eliminates the other options in its row and column.

When a clue says that two elements are not related, mark their square with a cross. If two squares in a row or column are crosses, the third must be a tick.

Mark:

Who rode the black horse?

Correct: Walter Scott dropped the coin

Hogg had the brooch and Shortreed had the watch, leaving the coin for Scott. The new dress clue places Hogg on the brown horse. Shortreed must therefore have ridden white, leaving the black horse for Scott. Scott dropped the coin.
