An 1896 Jedburgh mystery

The Body on the Bridge

The cold case

On a cold winter night in 1896, a scream was heard in Jedburgh. Early the next morning, Jock Wilson, a baker heading to work, spotted a body lying on Canongate Bridge.

Magistrates interviewed witnesses and rounded up four known criminals. Various items were recovered from the river below—perhaps including the murder weapon. No one was charged, convicted or punished.

Match every suspect with one motive, weapon and alibi. The killer is the suspect whose alibi was out walking alone.
