All Lies and Jest

Alan Renton always follows the same weekly pattern. Can you find the only day on which his statement makes sense?

The suspect’s strange routine

Alan Renton was in town at the time of the murder and immediately fell under suspicion. He was known for his strict routines: he checked locked doors repeatedly, washed his hands 30 times a day and told lies on specific days of the week.

At the time of the murder, Alan told the truth on Monday, Wednesday and Friday—and lied on every other day.

“Tomorrow I will tell the truth!”

On which day did Alan say this?

Choose a day, remembering that the statement itself must follow Alan’s truth-and-lie pattern.

Correct—Saturday

Saturday is a lying day, and Sunday is also a lying day. So when Alan says he will tell the truth tomorrow, his statement is false—exactly as it must be on Saturday.

  1. On a truth day, tomorrow would also have to be a truth day. None of Alan’s truth days is followed by another truth day.
  2. On a lie day, tomorrow must not be a truth day. Saturday is the only lie day followed by another lie day.
