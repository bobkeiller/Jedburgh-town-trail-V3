Reasons to Return

One trail is only the beginning.

If you completed the Essential Jedburgh Trail, why not come back and do the full trail?

If you completed Explore the Town, why not come back. collect the plaques and solve the puzzles and murder mysteries?

If you had limited time, why not make time to come back and visit our Abbey, the Jedburgh Castle Jail or Mary Queen of Scots House?

If you want to experience local culture, why not come back for one of these?

  • The annual Hand Ba’ is in March.
  • The Rugby Sevens are in May.
  • The Jethart Callants Festival is in July.
  • Edgefest is a full weekend of music in September.
  • Our Festival of Running is in October.
