A Jedburgh Friary logic mystery

The Demise of Father Ferguson

The year is 1607. Father Ferguson, leader of the Greyfriars, has been found dead—and three unusually tight-lipped witnesses know what happened.

The case

Forensic science was non-existent, so the magistrates must rely on the three monks who witnessed the event.

Each monk has given one statement—but they take turns telling the truth.

Exactly one monk is telling the truth. The other two are lying.

Monk 1

“It was the poisoned wine.”

Monk 2

“It was not the dagger.”

Monk 3

“It was not the wine.”

What killed Father Ferguson?

Choose the only weapon that makes exactly one statement true.

Try each weapon in turn. Count how many of the three statements would be true; you need a total of exactly one.

This playful mystery is a fictional puzzle inspired by Jedburgh’s Greyfriars.
