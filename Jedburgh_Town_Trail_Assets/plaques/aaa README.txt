Jedburgh Town Trail - extracted graphics and images

Source: official 30-page Scottish Borders Council PDF.

Contents
- photographs_native: 34 unique embedded raster images, saved without upscaling.
- maps_and_composite_graphics: the two centre-spread map pages and a 300 dpi stitched spread.
- branding_and_decorative_artwork: a 300 dpi cover render and the Scottish Borders Council mark.

Notes
- Repeated embedded images were deduplicated by pixel content.
- Very small texture tiles (under 100 px in either dimension) were omitted as layout artefacts.
- Photographs in the source are generally about 239 x 239 px; these files preserve the available source resolution.
- The map and cover contain vector/composite content, so they were rendered at 300 dpi to preserve appearance.
